# Start here

This kit describes one way to run a small company with Claude. It came out of a real build at a small company, and every rule in it exists because something went wrong without it. The company and the people are left out; the lessons are kept.

## The idea in one paragraph

Each person has their own Claude, connected to their own mail, calendar, files and task board. Every Claude reads the same company rulebook first. Scheduled runs do the daily grind: they read what came in, keep a list of what each person owes and is owed, draft the messages that close those loops, and build the day's agendas. People read, fix and send. A separate check makes sure every claim in a draft can be traced to a source before anyone acts on it.

## Three promises the system makes

1. Nothing goes to another person unless a human sends it.
2. Every number and every "this is done" shows where it came from.
3. If a run can't check something, it says so instead of guessing.

If your build breaks one of these, fix that before anything else.
