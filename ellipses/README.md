# Ellipses, a live meeting co-pilot

Website: [ellipsesapp.ai](https://ellipsesapp.ai) (described here as the site read on Nov 15, 2026)

The kit works before and after meetings: agendas in the morning, action items and drafts from the transcript afterwards. Ellipses is a separate app for the middle part, while the call is happening.

## What it does

You install your own copy inside your Claude account, and it runs on your own connectors. During a call it reads the live transcript every 20 to 45 seconds and shows up to three short cards each time: corrections, numbers, better phrasing, answers, and questions to ask. Cards are sorted into six kinds (urgent, check, answer, add, coach, and open commitments). Every card carries a source badge, and a figure with no source is marked unverified. Afterwards you can export your notes and takeaways.

It fits the kit's habits: claims show their source, unverified is labeled, and you decide what to say.

## What it needs

A Claude account that can publish artifacts and use connectors, and a live transcript source (the site says which one, and that you can paste text instead). Other connectors are optional and read only. The site says Ellipses is free to install and use, with optional donations. Check the site for current requirements and how it handles your data.

## How it relates to the kit

- It is not part of the kit's files. The kit links to it and describes it; it does not include the app.
- The kit's meeting rules still apply: tell other people on the call when a notetaker is on, and treat what Ellipses shows during the call as leads to check, not as receipts. Anything you quote or turn into a task afterwards comes from the verbatim transcript.
