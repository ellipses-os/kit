# Ellipses, a live call co-pilot

Website: [ellipsesapp.ai](https://ellipsesapp.ai)

The kit works before and after meetings: agendas in the morning, action items and drafts from the transcript afterwards. Ellipses is a separate app for the middle part, while the call is happening.

## What it does

During a live call, Ellipses reads the transcript as it's written and checks each new line against a fact sheet you keep. When something comes up that matters, it shows a short card: a fact that needs checking, a point worth clarifying, a better way to frame something, an answer to a question just asked, a new open loop, or a commitment someone just made. Anything it can't find on your fact sheet or in your connected tools is marked unverified, so you know not to repeat it as fact.

It fits the kit's habits: claims carry their source, unverified is labeled, and you decide what to say.

## How it relates to the kit

- It is not part of the kit's files. The kit links to it and describes it; it does not include the app.
- It uses its own setup. See the website for what it needs, how it works with your transcript source, and how it handles your data.
- The kit's meeting rules still apply: tell outside guests when a call is being transcribed, and treat what Ellipses shows during the call as leads to check, not as receipts. Anything you quote or turn into a task afterwards comes from the verbatim transcript.
